# Global Opportunity Connect

**Going abroad shouldn't depend on who you happen to know.** Global Opportunity Connect (also known as Global Pathways) is a platform that helps ambitious students and early-career people find real, trustworthy routes to study, live, and work abroad — and then walk those routes one clear step at a time.

Most people miss out on scholarships, student visas, and international jobs not because they aren't qualified, but because the information is scattered, the deadlines are hidden, and the process feels like guesswork. This platform exists to replace that guesswork with clarity.

---

## Who it's for

- **Students** looking for scholarships to fund their studies abroad.
- **Aspiring travellers** who need honest guidance on student visa routes.
- **Early-career talent** searching for graduate roles and jobs in other countries.

The current focus is opportunities across **Europe and Asia**, curated with a strong base in Rwanda and the wider region.

---

## What you can do here

**Discover opportunities.** Browse a curated library of scholarships, student visas, and jobs. Search by country, route, or keyword, and see each opportunity's deadline, eligibility, and required documents at a glance. Every listing links back to its **verified official source**, so you always know the information is real.

**Apply — with or without an account.** Start an application when you feel informed, not rushed. You can apply as a guest using just your email, or sign in to keep everything connected to a personal dashboard. Upload your certificates, passport, transcripts, or CV securely along the way.

**Track where you stand.** Your dashboard shows each application's status on a simple timeline — from submitted, through review, to a final decision — plus the opportunities you've saved for later. No refreshing your inbox and wondering; the next step is always visible.

**Pay the service fee easily.** When an application reaches the payment step, the small service fee is paid directly from your phone using **MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money**. You approve the prompt on your own device — nothing is charged without your say-so.

**Talk to a real guide.** Not sure which route fits your plans? Ask a question and a guide will help you orient yourself before you commit to anything.

---

## What we promise

- **Verified, not vague.** Opportunities point to their official sources with the date they were checked.
- **You're always in control.** We never submit an application or make a payment on your behalf without your explicit approval.
- **Your information is handled with care.** Personal details and documents are kept private and are never shared without your consent.
- **Open to everyone.** You don't need an account, a referral, or insider knowledge to get started — just a genuine ambition to go further.

> A considered, trustworthy path to your next chapter abroad — so the only thing standing between you and the opportunity is your decision to pursue it.
