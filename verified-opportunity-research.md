# Verified opportunity research

## Chevening Scholarships — United Kingdom
Official source: https://www.chevening.org/scholarships/application-timeline/

The official Chevening application timeline states that 2027–2028 scholarship applications opened on 4 August 2026 at 11:00 UTC and close on 6 October 2026 at 11:00 UTC. The page also states that applicants should apply through their country or territory page. This is a verified, currently open scholarship listing with a fixed official deadline.

## MEXT / Japanese Government Scholarship — Japan
Official source: https://www.studyinjapan.go.jp/en/planning/scholarships/mext-scholarships/

The official Study in Japan website states that MEXT applications use either embassy recommendation or university recommendation. It explicitly says that embassy recruitment, openings, detailed requirements, and schedules vary by country, and that applicants should check their Japanese embassy or consulate. University recommendation availability and requirements also vary by university. This should be cataloged as a verified scholarship route with a country-specific or institution-specific deadline rather than inventing one global deadline.

## Research policy for the catalog

Only listings with an official source page and an explicit deadline or clearly documented country-specific deadline policy should be marked verified. When the official source does not publish one universal deadline, the platform should show “Deadline varies — check official source” rather than fabricate a date. Source URL, verification date, and deadline note should be visible to staff and students.

## UN Careers — Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan
Official source: https://careers.un.org/jobSearchDescription/281339

The official UN Careers page lists “Representative, Human Rights, P-5,” Job Opening ID 281339, duty station Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan, posted 28 July 2026, with a deadline of 10 September 2026. The page provides the official Apply Now action and states that the role is with OHCHR and covers the Central Asia multi-country office. This is a verified current job listing with a fixed closing date at the time of research.

## EURES — European Union employment portal
Official source: https://eures.europa.eu/index_en

The official EURES portal describes itself as the European employment services portal and directs jobseekers to explore new jobs in Europe through country and regional searches. The landing page also states that new jobs are posted during the last seven days. Because individual vacancy deadlines are attached to individual live listings and change frequently, the platform should link to EURES search results or individual vacancy pages and avoid inventing a universal deadline. The catalog should mark these as dynamic official job search routes unless an individual vacancy has been captured and re-checked.

## DAAD Scholarship Database
Official source: https://www.daad.de/stipdb-redirect/

The official DAAD database says it contains information on DAAD scholarship programmes for international students and researchers plus selected external funding organisations. It displays many programme options and warns applicants to contact the named programme contact to confirm current terms before applying. Catalog representation: official scholarship database; deadline should be marked programme-specific unless a particular programme page is captured.

## EURAXESS Jobs and Opportunities
Official source: https://euraxess.ec.europa.eu/jobs

The official EURAXESS page states that it lists thousands of job opportunities from more than 43 countries and regions, with separate search actions for jobs and hosting offers. Catalog representation: official dynamic research-jobs portal; individual vacancy closing dates must be checked on the vacancy page rather than represented as a universal deadline.

## MYFutureJobs Malaysia
Official source: https://myfuturejobs.gov.my/

The official MYFutureJobs homepage presents a “Find jobs” action, free career services for jobseekers, job matching, labour-market information, career fairs, and candidate notifications. Catalog representation: official national employment portal; individual job deadlines should be checked on the vacancy page.

## Taiwan Scholarship
Official source: https://taiwanscholarship.moe.gov.tw/

The official Taiwan Scholarship and Huayu Enrichment Scholarship Office page identifies the Ministry of Education and Ministry of Foreign Affairs authorities, provides scholarship terms and university information bulletins, and lists official scholarship contact details. Catalog representation: official scholarship information route; application windows vary by Taiwan representative office and should not be represented as one universal deadline without a specific current bulletin.

## Careers@Gov Singapore
Official source: https://www.careers.gov.sg/

The official Singapore government careers site identifies itself as a .gov.sg agency site, links applicants to the official jobs portal at https://jobs.careers.gov.sg/, and describes public-service roles across ministries and statutory boards. Catalog representation: official public-service jobs route; each vacancy controls its own closing date.
